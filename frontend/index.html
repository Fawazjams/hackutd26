        What downtime costs

        Each hour a GPU is down costs its share of the H100 market price that day, scaled by how busy the hall is at that hour.

        Real weekly prices through Nov 2, daily forecast is our model. Data: GetDeploying, CC BY 4.0, forecast added.

        How it’s priced

        cost(hour) = GPUs offline × utilization(hour) × price(day) × (1 + SLA)
        + risk of waiting = failure chance(days waited) × unplanned cost

        In GPU cloud mode, downtime is lost revenue and the SLA term adds credits owed on contracted hours.In Private DC mode, downtime is what it costs to rent replacement GPUs; there is no SLA term.

        The planner tries every allowed start for every task, never overlaps two jobs in one cooling zone, and keeps the cheapest schedule. The baseline starts each job at the first business-hours slot.

        Honesty note. The facility, backlog, utilization and risk numbers are simulated or assumed. Prices: the history is real (GetDeploying weekly H100 on-demand median across ~46 providers, $4.02/hr on Nov 2, 2026); the day-by-day forecast for the planning week is our own trend model. A snapshot, not a live feed.
