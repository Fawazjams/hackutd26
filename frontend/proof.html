How we tested

  • Seeds 1 to 100. Each week has 5-7 jobs from the equipment catalog (CRAC, CDU, PDU, fiber, UPS) with random duration, failure-risk growth and allowed window.
  • On about 40% of weeks we plant a CRAC job and a CDU job in neighboring cooling zones. If they overlap, those zones lose backup cooling. The planner never sees this answer key.
  • Fixed: 40-rack, 4-zone hall, real-data H100 price forecast, cost model, thermal model.
  • Both plans are simulated hour by hour. Savings are compared only on the 93 weeks where both plans schedule the same jobs.

Honesty caveat

  • Every week runs on our assumed thermal and cost model. This shows the planner is robust inside our simulation, not in a real hall.
  • "Optimized never overheats" holds by construction, since it checks every slot against the simulator. The informative numbers are the ASAP catch rate, the savings and the unscheduled jobs.
  • Most of the saving is ASAP's overheats priced as outages at an assumed rate. The $218.66 figure is what timing alone saves.
